[#1] Dick Taylor (5★, 2 years ago)
"We've had Phil H tackle two plumbing projects in our home for us.

The first was a leaking pressure value on our hot water heater or so we thought. Phil diagnosed the problem as being the expansion tank installed by the City of Winchester in 2020. Sure enough, when Phil took the tank off, it was filled with water. Phil replaced both the tank and pressure valve on the water heater and it hasn't leaked since.

Next, we wanted to put in a new kitchen sink. Phil again, came and installed it and a new garbage disposal we had purchased. He encountered a problem with a led pipe Y-connector under the sink after everything had been installed. It leaked, not a lot but in two places; pin hole type leaks. So, he took everything he had just installed apart and found that 64-years of stuff going down the drain had not just corroded the pipe but had eaten it away and weakened the metal to the point you could push a hole into it with your finger. It was a problem just waiting to happen. Thank Goodness Phil found it and replaced the pipe causing the problem and put everything back in place.

The new sink and disposal are working perfectly and the water has never gone down the drain that fast since we've been living in this house the past ten years.

We can highly recommend Phil H and Pipe-It Plumbers."
